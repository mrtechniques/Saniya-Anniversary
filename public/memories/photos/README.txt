Place your personal anniversary photographs here using sequential filenames:
1.jpeg, 2.jpeg, 3.jpeg, ... through 26.jpeg

- 26.jpeg is used as the full-screen Hero cover photograph.
- 1.jpeg through 4.jpeg are paired with the four chapters in "Our Story".
- 5.jpeg through 25.jpeg are featured in "Our Memories" in numerical order.
- All 1.jpeg through 26.jpeg can be viewed in the fullscreen Lightbox.

If you have fewer or more photos, update `TOTAL_PHOTOS` in `src/config/anniversary.ts`.
